# To-Do List: Offroad Adventure Trailer

- [ ] CAD model the base frame (VKR profiles) and define bolt patterns for the corners.
- [ ] CAD design and simulate the corner reinforcements ("double sandwich") in 8-10 mm aluminum.
- [ ] Design CNC-milled niches for external electrical and gas outlets.
- [ ] Design mount/cradle for propane tank on the drawbar (including stone protection).
- [ ] Develop layouts for the electrical cabinet and network components (star ground, IP65 box).
- [ ] Design the water tank placement and aluminum tread skid plate.
- [ ] CAD model the kitchen drawer with space for the Dometic fridge and a safe cable chain for electricity/gas.

## Phase 2: Material Sourcing & Purchasing
- [ ] Order steel (VKR 50x50x3 or 60x40x3) and through-bolts (M10/M12 in 8.8/10.9).
- [ ] Buy 6082-T6 aluminum plates for corners and framework for CNC milling (PrintNC).
- [ ] Purchase torsion axle (bolt pattern 6x139.7) and Falken Wildpeak A/T3W 265/60R18 (with original rims).
- [ ] Order electrical components: IP65 cabinet, Circuit Breakers/RCD, Noco charger, DC-DC MPPT, Anderson connectors.
- [ ] Order network gear: Teltonika 5G, Poynting puck antenna, LMR coaxial cables.
- [ ] Purchase gas and water systems: 40L tank, 12V galley pump, GOK/Truma quick connects.
- [ ] Materials for body: Formply (12-15 mm), Dibond (3-4 mm), heavy-duty drawer slides.
- [ ] Consumables: Corrugated conduits, marine grease/Duralac, insulation, cable glands.

## Phase 3: Chassis Build & Mechanics

- [ ] Disassemble and send the steel frame for hot-dip galvanizing.
- [ ] Apply marine grease/Duralac and isolators (vinyl/rubber) between steel and aluminum.
- [ ] Final assembly of the chassis and torque the bolts.
- [ ] Mount axle, brakes, and wheels.
