# Concept: Gas & Water Systems

## Propane System (Right Side)
- **Safety Principle:** Completely separated from the left side to eliminate the risk of ignition in the event of an electrical fault.
- **Front Placement:** The P6/PK6 bottle stands safely behind a bent tread plate (stone guard) on the drawbar, secured in a CNC-milled cradle with heavy-duty straps.
- **External Outlets:** Two CNC-milled niches in the trailer's right Dibond side. The niches ensure that the quick connects (GOK/Truma with valve) do not protrude and get knocked off during tight forest driving. Perfect for a Muurikka griddle or a loose outdoor kitchen.
- **No Gas to the Kitchen Drawer:** Propane terminates at the fixed external quick-connects — nothing flexible, and nothing routed alongside electrical cables. The drawer's fridge is a 12V compressor unit (works off-level, no flame, no flue — an absorption fridge would need to be level and vented per EN 1949, which is incompatible with a slide-out), and the stove connects outdoors to the right-side quick-connect. The energy chain to the drawer carries **electrical only**, which keeps the left/right gas-electric separation intact all the way.

## Water System (Centered)
- **Center of Gravity:** The 40L tank weighs nearly 45 kg full. Placed exactly between the wheels (in front of/over the torsion axle) so as not to drastically affect the tongue weight during consumption.
- **Protection:** Suspended in a steel frame/straps under the trailer. Protected from below with a heavy-duty 3 mm aluminum tread plate (skid plate) to withstand rock strikes and stumps.
- **Pipe Routing:** A 12V galley pump is mounted indoors (frost-free/protected) on rubber mounts to absorb pump noise. Externally lockable filler cap in the Dibond wall.
- **Drainage:** An absolute requirement is a low point with a drain valve that can be opened from the side of the trailer before freezing temperatures.
